    About Navodaya Hospital

    When the need for quality healthcare in Kerala arose, the idea of Navodaya Hospital and Research Centre was born in the minds of its Directors. Built in the heart of central Pazhayannur, NHRC is open to all and aims at providing quality and affordable health care.

    The expertise, the commitment, the compassion and the infrastructure all come together to deliver technologically advanced care with a patient-focused approach.
