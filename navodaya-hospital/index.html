About Navodaya Hospital

Navodaya Hospital serves Pazhayannur and nearby communities with ethical, affordable and compassionate healthcare. Our team of experienced consultants, nurses and technicians works together so every patient gets the right diagnosis and the right treatment, close to home.

From routine check-ups to emergency and critical care, we combine modern equipment with a personal, family-like approach.

  • Experienced consultants in every speciality
  • Modern laboratory and digital imaging
  • Clean, safe and comfortable patient rooms
  • Insurance and cashless support at the help desk
